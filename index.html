Our Story

Years of Trusted Tool Supply

Burhani Tools was founded with a single mission: to make professional-grade tools accessible to every workshop, contractor and tradesperson in Pakistan.

We carry an extensive range from trusted Swiss and German brands alongside our own in-house manufactured line — designed specifically for the demands of the local market.

Whether you're a solo mechanic or running a large construction firm, we have the right tools at the right price — with the convenience of WhatsApp ordering and nationwide delivery.
